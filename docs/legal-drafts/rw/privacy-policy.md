## 1. Turi bande kandi iyi nyandiko ivuga iki?

Mountain Ceramic Company LTD ni yo ikoresha uru rubuga. Iyi nyandiko isobanura amakuru bwite ashobora gukoreshwa igihe ureba urubuga, wiyandikisha, ugura, utwandikira, ubika igishushanyo cy’icyumba, ucunga amahitamo ya kuki cyangwa wiyandikisha ku makuru y’ikigo. Ni umushinga w’igerageza, si inyandiko ya nyuma isobanura uburenganzira cyangwa inshingano z’amategeko.

## 2. Amakuru serivisi ikoresha

Bitewe n’uko ukoresha urubuga, serivisi ishobora gukoresha:

- **Konti no kwinjira:** amazina yawe, aderesi ya imeri, nimero ya telefone itari itegeko, amakuru yemeza ijambo ry’ibanga (si ijambo ry’ibanga ubwaryo risomeka), uko imeri yemejwe, inyandiko z’umutekano no kwinjira, n’amahitamo ya konti.
- **Kwemera inyandiko z’amategeko n’umutekano:** verisiyo z’inyandiko wemeye igihe wiyandikishaga, igihe wazemeye, aderesi ya IP na amakuru ya porogaramu ya mushakisha byandikwa hamwe n’icyo cyemezo, n’amakuru y’umutekano cyangwa agabanya ubutumwa bwinshi. Kugabanya ubutumwa bwinshi ku mpapuro zo kutwandikira bikoresha IP yahinduwe kode.
- **Ibyo watumije no kugezwa:** ibiri mu byo watumije, ibicuruzwa n’ingano, ibiciro, igabanyirizwa, ifaranga, amateka y’ibitumijwe, amakuru yo kukubona, aderesi n’amabwiriza yo kugezwa, nimero y’imisoro utanga ku bushake, n’uburyo n’uko ubwishyu bwagenze.
- **Inyandiko z’ubwishyu:** nimero z’ubucuruzi, telefone yishyuye igihe bibaye ngombwa, inyandiko z’amafaranga yasubijwe n’amakuru twakiriye ku mutanga serivisi yo kwishyura. Uburyo bwo kwishyura bushingira ku bugaragara kuri paji yo kwishyura. Inyandiko ya nyuma igomba kuvuga amazina y’abatanga serivisi nyakuri no kwemeza neza amakuru y’ubwishyu uru rubuga rwakira.
- **Ubutumwa n’ibishushanyo:** amakuru n’ubutumwa wohereje ukoresheje impapuro zo kutwandikira, ibisubizo, n’ibishushanyo cyangwa amahitamo yo kugerageza uko icyumba cyasa wahisemo kubika.
- **Amakuru y’isosiyete:** aderesi ya imeri, uko kwiyandikisha no kwemeza byagenze, igihe wemeye, n’inyandiko zo kwikuramo ku rutonde. Kwiyandikisha bisaba kubyemera ukwayo no kwemeza ukoresheje imeri.
- **Igikoresho, amahitamo n’imikoreshereze:** ururimi n’amakuru akenewe mu kwinjira, amahitamo ya kuki, inyandiko z’ibanze z’ibisabwa n’umutekano, ndetse—iyo ubyemeye gusa—amakuru y’imikoreshereze ya Google Analytics.

Ntutange kode y’ibanga y’ikarita ya banki, ijambo ry’ibanga rya konti cyangwa andi makuru y’ibanga mu butumwa bwo kutwandikira.

## 3. Uko dukoresha amakuru

Dukoresha amakuru mu gufungura no kurinda konti; kwemeza aderesi za imeri; gutanga ibikoresho byo ku rubuga wasabye; gusubiza ibibazo; gutegura no kugeza ibyo watumije; gutunganya kugezwa n’ubwishyu; kohereza ubutumwa bujyanye na serivisi nk’ukwemeza konti, ibyo watumije no guhindura ijambo ry’ibanga; kwandika ko wemeye inyandiko z’amategeko; gucunga urutonde rw’amakuru wemeye; gukumira ikoreshwa nabi n’uburiganya; kubungabunga serivisi; no kubahiriza inshingano z’amategeko akurikizwa.

Amakuru yo kwamamaza ari ku rutonde rw’isosiyete atandukanye n’ubutumwa bwa konti n’ibyo watumije. Ushobora kwikuramo ukoresheje umurongo uhabwa. Gusesengura uko urubuga rukoreshwa hakoreshejwe kuki si itegeko; bikorwa gusa iyo ubyemeye.

## 4. Impamvu z’amategeko n’amahitamo yawe

Impamvu y’amategeko ikwiye iterwa n’impamvu amakuru akenewe n’amategeko akurikizwa. Gukoresha amakuru bishobora kuba ngombwa kugira ngo dutange konti cyangwa serivisi wasabye, twubahirize inshingano y’amategeko, turinde serivisi, cyangwa bishobora gushingira ku kwemera kwawe igihe kubisabye (urugero, urutonde rw’amakuru n’isesengura ritari itegeko). Ikigo kigomba gusaba umunyamategeko kwemeza impamvu z’amategeko n’amagambo akoreshwa mu gusaba kwemera mbere yo gutangira. Ushobora gukuraho ukwemera kwawe ku bikorwa bizaza, bitagize ingaruka ku byo twakoze mu buryo bwemewe mbere yaho.

## 5. Kuki n’ububiko bwo kuri mushakisha

Urubuga rukoresha ububiko bwa mushakisha bukenewe kugira ngo winjire no kubika amahitamo wasabye. Kuki ya `mcc_cookie_consent` ibika amahitamo yawe ku isesengura mu gihe kitarenze iminsi 180. Google Analytics ikoreshwa gusa umaze kubyemera; igenamiterere ryayo rihisha igice cy’amakuru ya IP kandi rizimya amakuru y’iyamamaza ya Google. Iyo wanze isesengura, ntiratangira kandi kuki zaryo zirasibwa igihe mushakisha ibyemera.

Igikoresho cyo kugerageza uko icyumba cyasa gishobora kubika nimero y’igihe gito itamenyekanisha uwo uri we mu bubiko bwo kuri mushakisha kugira ngo gikomeze isomo ry’ibishushanyo. Ubundi bubiko bwo kuri mushakisha bushobora kubika by’igihe gito uko paji yari imeze. Ushobora guhindura amahitamo ya kuki ukoresheje ibikoresho byo ku rubuga cyangwa igenamiterere rya mushakisha yawe; kuzimya ububiko bukenewe bishobora kubuza kwinjira cyangwa kubika amahitamo.

## 6. Igihe dusangiza abandi amakuru

Ntidugurisha amakuru bwite. Amakuru ashobora kugerwaho n’abakozi babiherewe uburenganzira n’abatanga serivisi mu rugero rukenewe mu kubika urubuga n’ububiko bw’amakuru, kohereza imeri (harimo Resend), gutunganya ubwishyu, kugezwa, umutekano, isesengura wemeye no kugufasha. Abatanga serivisi bashobora gukoresha amakuru hakurikijwe amategeko yabo kandi mu bihugu bitari Rwanda. Mbere yo gutangira gukoresha urubuga nyarwo, ikigo kigomba kwemeza urutonde rw’abatanga serivisi nyakuri, amasezerano, uburyo bwo kurinda amakuru n’ibihugu akorerwamo.

Dushobora no gutanga amakuru iyo amategeko abidusabye cyangwa iyo bikenewe mu buryo bwumvikana kugira ngo turinde abantu, uburenganzira, umutungo cyangwa umutekano wa serivisi.

## 7. Igihe tubika amakuru n’umutekano

Tubika amakuru igihe akenewe ku mpamvu zasobanuwe haruguru, mu gucunga konti n’ibyo watumije, gukemura amakimbirane no kubahiriza inshingano z’amategeko n’ibaruramari. Gusiba konti ntibihita bisiba inyandiko z’ibitumijwe, ubwishyu, ukwemera inyandiko z’amategeko, umutekano cyangwa izindi amategeko asaba kubika. Ikigo kigomba gushyiraho no kwandika igihe nyacyo amakuru amara abitswe nyuma y’isuzuma ry’amategeko.

Dukoresha uburyo bugamije kurinda amakuru, harimo kubika ijambo ry’ibanga mu buryo bwihishe, kugenzura abemerewe kuyageraho no gukurikirana umutekano. Nta serivisi cyangwa kohereza amakuru kuri interineti bishobora kwizezwa ko bifite umutekano wuzuye.

## 8. Amahitamo yawe n’ibyo ushobora gusaba

Hakurikijwe amategeko akurikizwa, ushobora gusaba kubona amakuru yawe cyangwa kuyakosora, gusaba ko asibwa cyangwa ko imikoreshereze yayo igabanywa, kwanga uburyo bumwe bwo kuyakoresha cyangwa gukuraho ukwemera kwawe. Ushobora kandi kuvugurura amakuru ya konti, gusaba gusiba konti wandikira info@mountainceramic.com, gucunga amahitamo yawe ku isesengura no kwikura ku rutonde rw’amakuru. Dushobora kugenzura ko ari wowe kandi tugakomeza kubika amakuru amwe iyo amategeko abidusaba cyangwa abyemera.

## 9. Abana

Iyi serivisi ntiyagenewe gukusanya amakuru bwite y’abana. Ikigo kigomba gusaba umunyamategeko kwemeza imyaka isabwa n’amategeko n’amabwiriza agenga uruhushya rw’ababyeyi cyangwa ababarera. Niba utekereza ko umwana yatanze amakuru mu buryo budakwiye, twandikire.

Verisiyo iriho n’itariki yatangarijweho bizagaragazwa ku rubuga. Impinduka zikomeye zizakurikizwa hakurikijwe amategeko, harimo kongera gusaba ukwemera igihe bisabwa.
