# Amabwiriza yo gukoresha urubuga

> **INYANDIKO Y’IGERAGEZA — NTIRABONERWA UBWEMEZO BWO GUKORESHWA MU BURYO BUHORAHO.** Yateguwe ku wa 27 Ukwakira 2026 kugira ngo ifashe mu igerageza ryo kwiyandikisha. Umunyamategeko ubifitiye ubushobozi agomba kuyisuzuma no kwemeza ko ikwiye mbere yo kuyikoresha ku rubuga nyarwo. Kuyitangaza byandika ko uyikoresha yayemeye.

## 1. Ibyerekeye aya mabwiriza

Aya Mabwiriza yo gukoresha agenga uko winjira no gukoresha urubuga rwa Mountain Ceramic Company LTD, harimo konti z’abakiriya, kureba ibicuruzwa, impapuro zo kutwandikira, igikoresho cyo kugerageza uko ibyumba byasa n’uburyo bwo kwiyandikisha ku makuru y’isosiyete. Kugura ibicuruzwa na byo bigengwa n’Amategeko n’amabwiriza yo kugura atandukanye.

## 2. Konti

Tanga amakuru nyayo igihe wiyandikisha kandi ujye uyavugurura igihe bikenewe. Ni wowe ushinzwe kurinda ijambo ry’ibanga ryawe n’ibikorwa bikorewe kuri konti yawe. Tumenyeshe vuba niba ukeka ko hari undi wayinjiyemo atabyemerewe. Ntusangize abandi ijambo ry’ibanga kandi ntukoreshe konti y’undi muntu utabiherewe uburenganzira.

Dushobora kugusaba kwemeza aderesi yawe ya imeri, kandi dushobora guhagarika konti by’igihe gito bikenewe kugira ngo turinde konti, abakoresha cyangwa serivisi. Gusaba gusiba konti bishobora koherezwa kuri info@mountainceramic.com. Gusiba bishobora gutegereza ko ibyo watumije birangira cyangwa ko inyandiko amategeko asaba zibikwa zibitswe.

## 3. Imikoreshereze yemewe

Koresha urubuga mu buryo bwubahiriza amategeko kandi wirinde kurukoresha nabi. Ntugerageze kwinjira ahatemewe, kubangamira umutekano cyangwa imikorere y’urubuga, kwinjizamo porogaramu mbi, kohereza ubutumwa buriganya cyangwa butuka, cyangwa kuvogera uburenganzira bw’abandi. Ntukoreshe porogaramu zikora zonyine mu kuremerera urubuga cyangwa gukuramo amakuru aturutseho utabiherewe uburenganzira.

## 4. Ibyo utwoherereza

Ni wowe ugumana inshingano ku butumwa, amakuru yo kukubona, amazina y’ibishushanyo n’ibindi wohereje. Ntutwoherereze amakuru y’ibanga atari ngombwa kuri serivisi cyangwa ibintu utifitiye uburenganzira bwo gusangiza abandi. Uduha uburenganzira bwo kubika no gukoresha ibyo wohereje uko bikenewe kugira ngo dutange serivisi wasabye, tukugusubize, turinde serivisi kandi twubahirize inshingano z’amategeko. Ibi ntibihindura nyir’ibyo watwoherereje.

Ibishushanyo wabitse mu gikoresho cyo kugerageza uko icyumba cyasa bishobora guhuzwa na konti yawe. Ntidusezeranya ko ibyo bishushanyo cyangwa ibindi biri ku rubuga bizahoraho; bibike ahandi niba ubikeneye.

## 5. Urubuga n’uburenganzira ku bihangano

Urubuga, ikirango, inyandiko, amafoto y’ibicuruzwa, porogaramu n’ibindi biriho ni ibya Mountain Ceramic Company LTD cyangwa ikoreshwa ibifitiye uruhushya; birengerwa n’amategeko agenga uburenganzira ku bihangano. Ushobora gukoresha urubuga ku mpamvu zarwo zagenwe, iz’umuntu ku giti cye cyangwa iz’akazi. Ntukoporore, wongere utangaze, ugurishe cyangwa ukoreshe mu bucuruzi ibintu birengerwa utabiherewe uruhushya, keretse aho amategeko abyemera.

## 6. Serivisi z’abandi n’uko urubuga ruboneka

Urubuga rushobora kugira imiyoboro ijya kuri serivisi z’abandi cyangwa rukazikoresha, harimo izo kwishyura, kohereza imeri, gusesengura uko urubuga rukoreshwa cyangwa kwerekana ibindi bikoresho. Amategeko na politiki z’ibanga by’abo batanga serivisi ni byo bibagenga. Ntiducunga imbuga zo hanze. Dugerageza gutuma urubuga ruboneka, ariko ntitwemera ko ruzakora buri gihe nta guhagarara cyangwa nta kosa.

## 7. Guhagarika cyangwa kurangiza ikoreshwa

Dushobora kugabanya cyangwa guhagarika uburenganzira bwo gukoresha urubuga igihe bikenewe mu kurinda abakoresha, gusuzuma ikoreshwa nabi, kubahiriza amategeko cyangwa kubungabunga serivisi. Iyo bikwiye kandi byemewe n’amategeko, tuzasobanura impamvu tunaguhe uburyo bwo kutuvugisha. Ushobora kureka gukoresha urubuga cyangwa ugasaba ko konti yawe isibwa.

## 8. Inshingano n’uburenganzira butagomba kuvutswa

Nta giteganywa muri aya mabwiriza kivana cyangwa kigabanya uburenganzira cyangwa inshingano amategeko atemerera kuvana cyangwa kugabanya. Mu rugero amategeko abyemera, urubuga rutangwa uko ruboneka kandi nta sezerano ko buri gice cyarwo kizahoraho cyangwa ko kizaba kibereye buri mpamvu yose. Umunyamategeko agomba gusuzuma ingingo za nyuma zigabanya inshingano hakurikijwe amategeko akurikizwa.

## 9. Ivugurura no kutuvugisha

Verisiyo yatangajwe n’itariki yatangiye gukurikizwa bigomba kugaragazwa ku rubuga. Impinduka zikomeye zizakurikizwa nk’uko amategeko abiteganya. Ibibazo bijyanye n’aya mabwiriza byoherezwa kuri info@mountainceramic.com.
