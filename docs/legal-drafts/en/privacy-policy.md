## 1. Who we are and what this notice covers

Mountain Ceramic Company LTD operates this website. It is a testing draft, not a final statement of legal rights or obligations.

## 2. Information the service uses

Depending on what you do, the service may process:

- **Account and sign-in information:** first and last name, email address, optional phone number, password verifier (not your plain-text password), email-verification state, security and sign-in records, and account settings.
- **Legal acceptance and security data:** the versions of the registration documents you accept, acceptance time, IP address and browser user-agent recorded with that acceptance, and security or rate-limiting records. Contact-form rate limiting uses a hashed IP value.
- **Orders and delivery:** order contents, product and quantity details, prices, discounts, currency, order history, contact information, delivery address and instructions, optional tax identification number, and payment method and status.
- **Payment records:** transaction references, payer phone where applicable, refund records, and information returned by a payment provider. Payment options depend on what the site offers at checkout. The final notice must identify the actual providers and confirm exactly what payment data this site receives.
- **Messages and designs:** details and messages you submit through contact forms, replies, and room-visualizer designs or preferences you choose to save.
- **Newsletter:** email address, subscription and confirmation status, consent time, and unsubscribe records. Subscription requires a separate affirmative choice and email confirmation.
- **Device, preference, and usage data:** language and necessary sign-in preferences, cookie choices, basic request/security logs, and—only if you opt in—Google Analytics usage information.

Do not submit payment-card security codes, account passwords, or other sensitive information in a contact message.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; verify email addresses; provide requested website features; answer enquiries; prepare and fulfil orders; coordinate delivery and payment; send transactional messages such as verification, order, and password-reset emails; record legal-document acceptance; administer opted-in newsletters; prevent abuse and fraud; maintain the service; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Newsletter marketing is separate from account and order messages. You may unsubscribe from marketing messages using the link provided. Cookie-based analytics is optional and is loaded only after you enable it.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

The site uses necessary browser storage to support sign-in and requested preferences. The `mcc_cookie_consent` cookie records your analytics choice for up to 180 days. Optional Google Analytics is activated only after you opt in; the implementation configures anonymized IP and disables Google advertising signals. If you decline analytics, it is not loaded and analytics cookies are cleared where the browser permits.

The visualizer may keep an anonymous session identifier in browser local storage so a design session can continue. Other browser storage may be used for temporary interface state. You can change cookie settings from the site controls or use your browser settings; disabling necessary storage may prevent sign-in or preferences from working.

Information may be made available to authorized staff and service providers only as needed for hosting and database operations, email delivery (including Resend), payment processing, delivery, security, analytics you enable, and support. Providers may process information under their own terms and may operate in countries other than Rwanda. The company must confirm its actual provider list, contracts, safeguards, and processing locations before production.

Account deletion does not necessarily remove order, payment, legal-acceptance, security, or other records that must be retained. Exact retention periods must be set and documented by the company after legal review.

We use safeguards intended to protect information, including password hashing, access controls, and security monitoring. No online service or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.
